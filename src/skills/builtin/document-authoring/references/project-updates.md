# Progress, plans, problems

Adapted from Anthropic internal-comms/examples/3p-updates.md (Apache-2.0).
VCP changes: optional format, scoped source gathering, corrected past-period
wording, no mandatory emoji or broad connected-account search.

3P updates stand for Progress, Plans, Problems. Write for executives, leadership
or teammates who have some context, with enough detail to read in 30–60 seconds.
The larger the team, the less granular the individual tasks should be.

- Progress: what the team accomplished in the reporting period: shipped work,
  achieved milestones and completed tasks.
- Plans: the highest-priority work for the next reporting period.
- Problems: blockers or risks that are slowing the team, with the required
  decision or help where known.

Establish the team and reporting period from supplied context. Gather facts from
the authorized task sources; do not search unrelated accounts or send messages
merely because a connector exists. If a material fact is unavailable, mark it
unknown or ask a focused question instead of making it up.

When the user has no other format, use the team name and dates followed by those
three labels, each with one to three concise sentences. Include metrics when
known and useful, with source links. Distinguish completed work from work merely
started. Review the update for factual support and usefulness to its audience.
