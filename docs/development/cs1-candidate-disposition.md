# CS-1 non-default candidate disposition

On September 25, 2026 the owner approved delivering document-authoring and
skill-authoring 1.0.1 as **non-default, unqualified candidates**, together with
their evaluation harness and retained results. CS-1 acceptance remains unmet.
The owner then requested a good-enough closure: finish the focused checks and
merge the three delivery PRs, retain qualification as deferred, and stop this work.
No further refinement or evaluation campaign is required for this disposition.

The packages live under `src/skills/candidates/`. Their descriptors, bodies and
resource bytes are unchanged from the frozen 1.0.1 diagnostic. This directory
is a repository convention, not an automatic loader. The default catalog retains
21 skills and 44 packaged files; neither candidate is shipped in its inventory.
Candidate use requires an explicit existing skill source and explicit selection.
Selection does not grant tools or effects, and integrity tests do not establish
usefulness.

The [full diagnostic outcome](cs1-full-diagnostic-outcome.md) retains the DOC
quality failures, authority violation, permanent halt, unused slots and closed
accounting: 181 settled requests and $0.401397 across the stated successor
campaigns. SKL 1.0.1 has no new v3 comparison result. The earlier
[DOC](cs1-document-comparison.md) and [SKL](cs1-skill-comparison.md) reports retain
their separate 1.0.0 history and accounting. Frozen worktrees, receipts and
campaign inputs remain unchanged; old catalog and executable identities describe
only the artifacts actually tested.

The [canonical tool ceiling](p2-canonical-tool-ceiling.md) corrects the prospective
runtime advertisement/admission mismatch. It cannot repair a frozen result or
explain away independent document quality defects. Future candidate preparations
must bind their explicit source identities and current runtime; historical
authorizations do not transfer to changed plans. This disposition authorizes no
new paid probe, comparison, replay or allocation of unused slots.

Default promotion still requires the original [CS-1 exit criteria](../plan/24-skills-follow-on.md#cs-1--authoring-foundation),
including independent comparison benefit, all preservation/authority gates and
exact native package checks. CS-2 through CS-7 are not completed by merging these
candidates. The implemented [Node adapter prerequisite](cs2-node-fixture-adapter.md)
is separately bounded; browser and document/data adapter acceptance remains
outstanding. P10-04, M10, other Markov experiments and retained P8 qualification
gaps keep their existing deferred status.
