corrections. They do not inherit qualification, owner acceptance or evaluation
results from earlier package identities. Both remain outside the builtin catalog
under `src/evals/skills/authoring-requalification/candidates/` and require explicit

under `src/skills/candidates/` remain byte-identical.

atomic source-coverage list, exact contract units, complete acceptance surfaces
and a final reread of exact returned file bytes. This targets the missing naming,
scenario, provenance and status facts plus the reported trailing-LF mismatch.

correct artifacts, but narrows native verification citations to current complete
same-task reads. It permits one configured verifier attempt and requires a
fail-closed stop on a missing or conflicting store record. This does not claim the
store defect is fixed; a new feasibility receipt must prove that boundary before
another live comparison.
