# VCP code of conduct

VCP welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and accessible.

## Participation standards

- Discuss ideas and changes on their merits. Give specific, useful feedback without personal attacks.
- Be considerate of other people's time, communication needs, and experience. Ask for context before assigning motives.
- Respect names, pronouns, privacy, and personal boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct misleading statements, and follow reasonable moderation requests.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information without consent are unacceptable. Repeated disruption, unwanted contact after being asked to stop, and retaliation against someone reporting a concern are also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents VCP in a public venue. Technical disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@ioka.io** with `VCP conduct` in the subject. Include links or a concise description of the incident, relevant dates, and your preferred contact method. Share only the personal information needed to review the report. Do not open a public issue containing private allegations or personal details.

Project maintainers review reports and limit access to information to those handling the matter, subject to any applicable obligations. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome. Reporters may identify a conflict of interest or request another reviewer through the same channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude someone from project spaces. The response should account for the behavior's severity, impact, and any repeated violations. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate exclusion.

When appropriate, maintainers explain the decision and conditions for continued participation to the affected person without exposing private reports. Requests to reconsider a decision can be sent to the reporting address with relevant new information.
